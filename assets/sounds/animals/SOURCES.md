# Animal sound sources

- `dog.mp3`: “Ladrido perro.ogg” by Edo.pt2, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ladrido_perro.ogg
- `sheep.mp3`: “Sheep bleating.ogg” by earthcalling, released into the public domain. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sheep_bleating.ogg

The bundled MP3 files are Wikimedia Commons transcodes. Playback is limited by the application to approximately two seconds per interaction.
